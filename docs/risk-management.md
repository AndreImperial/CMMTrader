# Risk Management

Current controls include:

- Paper-mode only default.
- Maximum position notional.
- Maximum daily loss.
- Minimum confidence.
- Minimum risk/reward.
- Maximum stop distance versus ATR.
- Maximum ATR percentage.
- BTC regime gatekeeping.
- Liquidity floors.

Remaining work:

- Central machine-readable rejection codes.
- Risk-per-trade sizing from stop distance.
- Portfolio-level risk limits.
- Correlated exposure limits.
- Drawdown lockout.
- Maximum trades per hour.
- Maximum losses per day.
- Exchange outage lockout.

Live execution remains out of scope until these controls are complete and tested.
